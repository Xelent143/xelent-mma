## Company Credentials

### About Xelent MMA

Xelent MMA is a premium fightwear manufacturer based in Sialkot, Pakistan - the global hub for sports goods manufacturing. With over a decade of experience in crafting Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu gi, MMA gear, and combat sports equipment, we combine traditional Pakistani craftsmanship with modern athletic performance standards.

**Founded:** 2012
**Headquarters:** Sialkot, Punjab, Pakistan
**Industry:** Combat Sports Equipment Manufacturing
**Specialization:** BJJ Gi, Rashguards, MMA Shorts, Fightwear

### Our Manufacturing Heritage

Sialkot, Pakistan has been renowned for sports goods manufacturing since the 1800s. The city produces over 70% of the world's footballs and is home to some of the most skilled leatherworkers and textile craftsmen in the industry. Xelent MMA leverages this centuries-old tradition of excellence to produce fightwear that meets the highest international standards.

**Certifications:**
- ISO 9001:2015 Quality Management System
- SEDEX Certified (Ethical Manufacturing)
- OEKO-TEX Standard 100 (Textile Safety)
- IBJJF Compliance Testing

### Quality Control Process

Every Xelent MMA product undergoes rigorous quality control:

1. **Material Selection:** Premium cotton, polyester, and spandex sourced from certified suppliers
2. **Pre-Production Sampling:** Multiple prototype iterations for fit and function
3. **In-Line Inspection:** Quality checks at every manufacturing stage
4. **Final Inspection:** 100% inspection of finished goods before packaging
5. **Durability Testing:** Stress testing, wash testing, and wear simulation
