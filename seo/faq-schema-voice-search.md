### 2. How do I wash my BJJ gi properly?

**Short Answer:**
Wash your BJJ gi in cold water with mild detergent, air dry or tumble dry on low heat. Never use bleach or fabric softeners. Turn the gi inside out before washing to protect the fabric. Wash after every training session to prevent bacteria buildup and odor. Hang dry in a well-ventilated area for best results.

**Full Answer:**
Proper gi care extends its lifespan and maintains hygiene:
1. **After training:** Air out your gi immediately - don't leave it in your bag
2. **Pre-wash:** Remove any visible stains with cold water
3. **Washing:** Cold water (30°C/86°F max), mild detergent, gentle cycle
4. **Drying:** Air dry preferred; tumble dry low if necessary
5. **Frequency:** Wash after every training session
6. **Never:** Use bleach, hot water, or fabric softeners
7. **Storage:** Ensure completely dry before storing to prevent mold

### 3. What does IBJJF approved gi mean?

**Short Answer:**
An IBJJF approved gi meets the International Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu Federation's strict competition standards for colors, measurements, and materials. Approved gis must be white, black, or royal blue, have sleeves that reach within 5cm of the wrist, and be made of cotton or cotton-like fabric. The gi must also have proper thickness and cannot have patches outside permitted areas.

**Full Answer:**
IBJJF approval ensures your gi is legal for IBJJF-sanctioned competitions:
- **Colors:** Only white, royal blue, or black are permitted
- **Material:** Cotton or cotton-like fabric required
- **Sleeve length:** Must reach within 5cm of the wrist bone when arms are extended
- **Skirt length:** Must reach within 5cm of the ankle bone
- **Collar thickness:** Maximum 1.3cm when folded
- **Patches:** Only allowed in designated areas (front, sleeves above elbow, pants above knee)
- **Belt:** Must match rank with black tip for blue belt and above

Always check the latest IBJJF rules as regulations can change.

### 6. What size BJJ gi should I buy?

**Short Answer:**
BJJ gi sizes follow an "A" (adult) system from A0 to A5, based primarily on height with weight as a secondary consideration. Measure your height and compare to the manufacturer's size chart. If between sizes, choose based on your build - size down for a competition fit or up for a looser training fit. Most gis will shrink slightly after the first few washes.

**Full Answer:**
Standard sizing guide:
- **A0:** 5'0"-5'4" (152-163cm) | 110-140 lbs (50-63kg)
- **A1:** 5'4"-5'8" (163-173cm) | 140-170 lbs (63-77kg)
- **A2:** 5'8"-6'0" (173-183cm) | 170-200 lbs (77-91kg)
- **A3:** 6'0"-6'3" (183-191cm) | 200-230 lbs (91-104kg)
- **A4:** 6'3"-6'5" (191-196cm) | 230-260 lbs (104-118kg)
- **A5:** 6'5"+ (196cm+) | 260+ lbs (118kg+)

Consider pre-shrunk vs shrink-to-fit options. Some brands offer "L" (long) or "H" (husky) variations for non-standard body types.

### 7. What is the difference between pearl weave and gold weave BJJ gi?

**Short Answer:**
Pearl weave is lighter (350-450gsm), cooler, and preferred for competition and hot climates. Gold weave is heavier (550-950gsm), more durable, and ideal for daily training and rough grappling. Pearl weave has a smaller, tighter pattern while gold weave features larger loops and a thicker feel. Most practitioners own both types for different training conditions.

**Full Answer:**
**Pearl Weave:**
- Weight: 350-450gsm (lightweight)
- Pattern: Small, tight weave resembling pearls
- Best for: Competition, hot weather, travel
- Feel: Softer, more flexible
- Drying: Quick-drying

**Gold Weave:**
- Weight: 550-950gsm (heavyweight)
- Pattern: Larger, open loops
- Best for: Daily training, durability, winter
- Feel: Stiffer, more rigid
- Drying: Takes longer to dry

**Single Weave:** Budget option, lighter than gold
**Double Weave:** Two layers for maximum durability
**Platinum/Ripstop:** Advanced weaves for specific needs

### 9. Can I use a Judo gi for BJJ?

**Full Answer:**
**Judo gi differences:**
- **Cut:** Looser, baggier fit designed for Judo throws
- **Sleeves:** Wider sleeves harder to control in BJJ
- **Collar:** Thicker, stiffer collar
- **Skirt:** Longer jacket skirt
- **Fabric:** Often heavier and stiffer
- **Regulations:** May not pass IBJJF inspection

**When to use a Judo gi:**
- Beginner training before investing in BJJ gi
- Cross-training in both arts
- Casual practice where competition rules don't apply

**Recommendation:** Get a BJJ-specific gi if you're serious about training.

### 13. How do I prevent my BJJ gi from shrinking?

**Short Answer:**
To prevent BJJ gi shrinkage, wash in cold water (never hot), avoid high heat drying, and remove from the dryer while slightly damp. Buy pre-shrunk gis when possible, as most shrinkage occurs in the first 3-5 washes. If air drying, hang the gi fully extended to maintain proper shape and length.

**Full Answer:**
**Prevention strategies:**
1. **Buy pre-shrunk:** Look for "pre-shrunk" or "sanforized" labels
2. **Cold water only:** Never exceed 30°C (86°F)
3. **Air dry:** Best method; hang with sleeves extended
4. **Low heat:** If machine drying, use lowest heat setting
5. **Don't over-dry:** Remove while slightly damp and air finish
6. **Stretch when wet:** Gently pull fabric to maintain length

**If your gi shrinks:**
- Soak in lukewarm water with conditioner
- Gently stretch while wet
- Hang with weights to extend length
- Some shrinkage is irreversible - plan accordingly

### 15. How tight should a BJJ gi fit?

**Short Answer:**
A BJJ gi should fit snugly but not restrict movement. The jacket sleeves should reach your wrists, pants should touch your ankles, and you should be able to fully extend your arms overhead without the gi riding up excessively. For competition, the fit should be tighter to prevent opponents from gripping excess fabric. For training, a slightly looser fit is more comfortable.

**Full Answer:**
**Proper fit guidelines:**
- **Jacket:** Hem reaches mid-thigh, sleeves to wrist bone
- **Pants:** Hem at ankle bone, drawstring secures at waist
- **Collar:** Lays flat without gaping
- **Range of motion:** Can execute all techniques freely

**Competition vs Training fit:**
- **Competition:** Tighter, closer to minimum legal measurements
- **Training:** Slightly looser for comfort during long sessions
- **Shrinking:** Account for 5-10% shrinkage if not pre-shrunk

**Signs of poor fit:**
- Sleeves too short (illegal in competition)
- Jacket rides up when arms raised
- Pants fall down without tight drawstring
- Can't close lapels properly

### 16. What is the difference between BJJ gi and no-gi training?

**Short Answer:**
BJJ gi training uses the traditional kimono uniform with grips on the jacket, pants, and belt. No-gi training uses rashguards and shorts without the gi, focusing on wrestling-style grips on the body. Gi training emphasizes technical precision and grip fighting, while no-gi is faster-paced and more athletic. Both are valuable and most practitioners train both.

**Full Answer:**
**Gi Training:**
- **Uniform:** Kimono (gi) with belt
- **Grips:** Collar, sleeve, pant leg, belt grips
- **Pace:** Generally slower, more methodical
- **Technique emphasis:** Grip fighting, lapel chokes
- **Defense:** More escape options using gi grips

**No-Gi Training:**
- **Uniform:** Rashguard and shorts/spats
- **Grips:** Neck, wrists, joints, underhooks, overhooks
- **Pace:** Faster, more scrambles
- **Technique emphasis:** Wrestling, leg locks, body locks
- **Defense:** Requires different escape strategies

**Training recommendation:**
Train both styles to become a complete grappler. Many techniques translate between the two.

### 17. How do I remove stains from my BJJ gi?

**Short Answer:**
Remove BJJ gi stains by treating immediately with cold water and mild detergent. For blood stains, use cold water only (hot water sets blood stains). For grass or dirt, pre-soak in oxygen-based cleaner. For stubborn stains, apply a paste of baking soda and water before washing. Never use bleach as it weakens fabric fibers and causes yellowing.

**Full Answer:**
**Stain removal guide:**

**Blood stains:**
1. Rinse immediately with cold water
2. Apply hydrogen peroxide or enzyme cleaner
3. Let sit 10-15 minutes
4. Wash in cold water

**Sweat/yellowing:**
1. Soak in oxygen-based cleaner (OxiClean)
2. Add baking soda to wash
3. Sun dry (natural whitening)

**Mat burns/dirt:**
1. Pre-treat with stain remover
2. Use enzyme-based detergent
3. Wash in warm (not hot) water

**Preventive care:**
- Wash immediately after training
- Don't let stains set
- Rotate between multiple gis

### 20. How do I know if my BJJ gi is competition legal?

**Short Answer:**
Check if your BJJ gi is competition legal by measuring sleeve length (must reach within 5cm of wrist), pant length (within 5cm of ankle), verifying the color (white, blue, or black only), and ensuring the fabric is cotton or similar. Try it on and extend your arms fully - if sleeves rise significantly above the wrist, it may be too short. When in doubt, contact the tournament organizer or use IBJJF's official gi inspection guidelines.

**Full Answer:**
**Self-inspection checklist:**
1. **Color check:** Is it white, royal blue, or black?
2. **Sleeve test:** Extend arms - sleeves should be within 5cm of wrist
3. **Pant test:** Pants should be within 5cm of ankle
4. **Collar thickness:** Fold collar - should be under 1.3cm
5. **Patch placement:** Only on allowed areas
6. **Fabric feel:** Cotton or cotton-blend (not shiny synthetic)

**Pre-competition preparation:**
- Try on gi 2 weeks before competition
- Wash and shrink test if new
- Have a backup gi ready
- Bring gi to weigh-ins if possible

**When uncertain:**
Contact tournament officials with photos of your gi.
