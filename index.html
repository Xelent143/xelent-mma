Our Heritage

Crafted in Sialkot,
Worn Worldwide

For over a century, Sialkot has been the world's premier manufacturing hub for sporting goods. Xelent continues this legacy, combining traditional Pakistani craftsmanship with modern fightwear innovation.

Every Xelent gi passes through the hands of master artisans who have honed their craft for generations. From the pearl weave cotton to the reinforced stitching, quality isn't just a promise — it's our heritage.
