About the Elite Pro Gi

The Elite Pro Gi represents the pinnacle of our craftsmanship. Designed in collaboration with world-class athletes, this gi delivers uncompromising performance for training and competition.

Key Features

  • Premium Pearl Weave: 450gsm pearl weave fabric for optimal balance of durability and comfort
  • Reinforced Stitching: Triple-stitched stress points for maximum durability
