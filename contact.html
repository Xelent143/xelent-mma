FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does shipping take?
Domestic orders within Pakistan typically arrive within 3-5 business days. International orders take 7-14 business days depending on the destination. Express shipping options are available at checkout.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy for unworn items in original packaging. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, contact our support team to initiate a return or exchange.
Do you offer wholesale pricing?
Yes! We offer competitive wholesale pricing for gyms, retailers, and distributors. Please contact us via the wholesale inquiry option above for more information and pricing.
How do I find my gi size?
Use our size guide available on each product page. Measure your height and weight to find the best fit. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a looser fit or down for a competition fit.
