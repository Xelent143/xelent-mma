OUR MISSION

Xelent MMA was founded in 2019 by a group of passionate MMA practitioners in Sialkot, Pakistan. Frustrated by the high cost of quality gear and the lack of durable options, we set out to create something better.

Our mission is simple: provide premium BJJ and MMA gear at accessible prices without compromising on quality. Every product we make is tested by our own team on the mats before it ever reaches you.

Today, we're proud to serve fighters across 40+ countries, from white belts taking their first class to black belts competing on the world stage.
